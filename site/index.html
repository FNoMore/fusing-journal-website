A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before your
next firing.

What can I keep in a project?

Objectives, design notes, glass and mold usage, firing records and steps, results, lessons learned, difficulty, and captioned photos. Projects can be searched, duplicated, and saved as PDFs.

Do I need an internet connection?

Local journaling works offline. Signing in, syncing, uploading photos, and refreshing online catalogs need a connection. Sync is a manual action, rather than a background process.

Can I reuse firing schedules?

The app can download available shared schedule templates and cache them for offline use. A selected template is copied into your firing record, so later catalog changes do not rewrite your saved steps. Always review a schedule for your glass, project, and kiln.

Where can I download Fusing Journal?

App Store and Google Play links have not been added yet. This website introduces the app and lets you explore an illustrative project preview.
